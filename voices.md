# Voices & gender

- The voice (TTS) and the persona's grammatical gender must match: a male voice speaks masculine Hebrew (אֲנִי מִתְקַשֵּׁר), a female voice feminine (אֲנִי מִתְקַשֶּׁרֶת).
- Changing the voice usually means changing the name too (e.g. מִיקָה → a male name).
- Voices are usually fixed per call – you can't switch voice mid-call. To compare voices, make one short test call per voice and have it **say the voice's name** ("this is voice Adrian").
- Keep the recordings of the test calls to compare.
